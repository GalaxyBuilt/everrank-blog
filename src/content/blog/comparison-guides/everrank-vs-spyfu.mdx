# What Is SpyFu?

SpyFu is a competitor intelligence and keyword research platform focused heavily on:

- Historical keyword data  
- PPC ad tracking  
- Competitor domain research  
- SERP analysis  
- Keyword ranking history  

It is widely used by:

- PPC advertisers  
- Affiliate marketers  
- Agencies running Google Ads  
- SEOs focused on competitor keyword discovery  

SpyFu is strong at showing:

- What keywords competitors rank for  
- What ads they’ve run historically  
- Estimated traffic value  

However, SpyFu does not function as a content production system.

It gives data.

You still have to execute manually.
