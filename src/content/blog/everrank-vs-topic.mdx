# EverRank vs Topic: AI-Driven SEO for Modern Teams

Topic is a popular AI content optimization platform designed for **SEO-driven content creation**. It helps writers structure content, optimize for keywords, and analyze competitors. While it excels at **content briefs and optimization**, it **lacks integrated workflow automation and social distribution**.  

EverRank, on the other hand, is a **comprehensive AI SEO platform** combining **content creation, social posting, predictive analytics, and multi-site dashboards**, giving teams **all-in-one execution power**.

---

# Overview of Topic

Topic provides:

- AI content briefs with keyword recommendations  
- Competitor content analysis and gap identification  
- Content optimization scoring (SEO, readability, engagement)  

**Strengths:**

- Structured content briefs for writers  
- Clear optimization scoring and recommendations  
- Competitor content benchmarking  

**Weaknesses:**

- Limited AI content generation directly (depends on export to CMS)  
- No integrated social media posting  
- Limited workflow automation  
- No RocketFuel or credit system  

Topic is primarily **content-focused**, requiring external systems for publishing, distribution, and analytics.

---

# Overview of EverRank

EverRank provides:

- **DraftIQ AI**: generate 20–500+ articles/month depending on tier  
- AI social posts: fully automated promotion workflow  
- Predictive SEO & multi-site dashboards  
- Real-time website audits with actionable recommendations  

EverRank is designed for **actionable execution**, turning insights into immediate content and promotional output.

# Workflow Comparison

**Topic Workflow:**

1. Generate content briefs  
2. Export to CMS or Google Docs  
3. Write or have writers create content  
4. Optimize manually with Topic scoring  
5. Publish and track SEO externally  

**EverRank Workflow:**

1. AI competitor research & keyword insights  
2. Generate DraftIQ articles directly  
3. Auto-create AI social posts for promotion  
4. Optimize pages and monitor ranking in-platform  
5. Multi-site dashboards for scaling teams  
6. Full feedback loop and predictive SEO analysis  

EverRank reduces **multi-tool friction**, allowing SEO teams to **research, create, publish, and promote** without leaving the platform.
