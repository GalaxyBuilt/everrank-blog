## 1. Getting Started with EverRank

1. **Sign Up & Select Your Tier**  
   EverRank offers four tiers: Starter ($119/mo), Pro ($299/mo), Agency ($899/mo), Enterprise ($1,499/mo). Each includes AI article quotas, social post allocations, competitor tracking, and access to different LLM models.

   > Tip: Starter gives enough credits to test article generation, audits, and basic rank tracking.

2. **Connect Your Website**  
   - Add your domain in the dashboard.  
   - Verify ownership via DNS, meta tag, or Google Search Console.  
   - This allows EverRank to scan your site and provide detailed health scores.

3. **Set Up Keywords & Competitors**  
   - Input your primary keywords and target phrases.  
   - Add competitors based on your tier: 3 in Starter, 25 in Pro, 100 in Agency, unlimited in Enterprise.  
   - EverRank automatically tracks rankings, search volume changes, and traffic impact.

> For a deeper dive into foundational keyword strategy, see [SEO Fundamentals & Best Practices](/blog/seo-fundamentals).

## 2. Performing Your First Site Audit

EverRank’s **AI-driven audits** evaluate technical SEO, content gaps, and performance:

- **On-Page SEO:** Meta tags, headings, content structure, internal links.  
- **Off-Page SEO:** Snapshot of backlink quality and outreach opportunities.  
- **Technical SEO:** Detects broken links, duplicate content, Core Web Vitals, and mobile responsiveness.

> Action: Run your first audit and resolve any high-priority issues immediately. Learn more about structured optimization in [Website & App Optimization](/blog/tech-optimization).

## 3. Generating AI Content with DraftIQ

EverRank integrates **DraftIQ** to generate AI-optimized content:

1. Navigate to the DraftIQ section.  
2. Enter your target keyword or topic.  
3. Set tone, word count, and SEO scoring preferences.  
4. Generate drafts — then review for accuracy, style, and internal links.

> Tip: DraftIQ can also suggest new subtopics to strengthen your [Growth Systems & Ranking Strategy](/blog/growth-systems).
